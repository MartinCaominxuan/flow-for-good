LET’S GET THE WATER FLOWING

One turn at a time.

  1. Tap a pipe to rotate it clockwise. Source and jerry can stay fixed.
  2. Connect all openings from the top-left source to the bottom-right can.
  3. Pollution makes water cloudy. A natural filter makes it clean again. These tiles stay fixed.
  4. Press FLOW WATER to test your route. Only clean water wins.

Levels 1–2 count rotations. Levels 3–4 use a timer. Level 5 uses both. The timer starts on your first action. Failed flows preserve your board. After a limit, keep practicing or restart the challenge.
